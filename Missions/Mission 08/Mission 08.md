## 1. DESTINATION

CompTIA Security+ V7

Mission 08 is designed to demonstrate specific Security+ objectives through security governance, risk management, compliance, and third-party/vendor risk. All atomic requirements are accounted for in the Atlas Register. This Passport must align with the master coverage map.

Cost Constraint: All labs executable with 100% free tools (policy templates, compliance frameworks, open-source risk management tools, document repositories).

---

## 2. MISSION INTENT

Understand how to establish security governance, manage organizational risk, ensure compliance, and oversee third-party relationships.

The mission begins with this organizational question:

"How do we ensure the organization's security posture is managed, measured, and aligned with business objectives?"

The objective is to develop the ability to:

|Skill|Purpose|
|---|---|
|Develop security governance structures|Define policies, standards, procedures|
|Implement risk management frameworks|Identify, assess, analyze, treat risk|
|Maintain compliance programs|Map controls to regulatory requirements|
|Manage third-party/vendor risk|Assess, monitor, and oversee vendors|
|Conduct audits and assessments|Evaluate security controls|
|Establish security awareness programs|Train employees on security|
|Document roles and responsibilities|RACI matrices, job descriptions|
|Produce tangible evidence of learning|Portfolio artifacts|
|Map demonstrated knowledge to Security+|Produce exam-ready evidence|

Security+ concepts will be introduced when they become necessary to answer questions about security program management.

---

## 3. SCENARIO

An organization has grown rapidly and now faces governance challenges:

- No unified security policies exist
- Risk management is reactive rather than proactive
- Compliance requirements are unclear or untracked
- Third-party vendors lack security oversight
- Security awareness training is inconsistent
- Roles and responsibilities are undefined

The mission is to build the security program infrastructure that provides:

`BUSINESS OBJECTIVES ↓ SECURITY GOVERNANCE (Policies, Standards) ↓ RISK MANAGEMENT (Identification, Assessment, Treatment) ↓ COMPLIANCE PROGRAM (Frameworks, Audits) ↓ THIRD-PARTY RISK (Vendor Assessment, Monitoring) ↓ SECURITY AWARENESS (Training, Reporting) ↓ OVERSIGHT MECHANISMS (Reporting, Metrics) ↓ SECURITY+ COVERAGE RECORD`

This contrasts with Missions 01–07 (technical implementation) by focusing on organizational management, policy, and oversight.

## 6. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Exploration occurs across multiple zoom levels:

### LEVEL 0 — Organizational View

`BOARD/EXECUTIVES ↓ SECURITY LEADERSHIP (CISO) ↓ SECURITY PROGRAM ↓ EMPLOYEES & VENDORS`

### LEVEL 1 — Security Program Components View

`┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ GOVERNANCE LAYER │ │ • Policies (High-level directives) │ │ • Standards (Required technical specifications) │ │ • Procedures (Step-by-step instructions) │ │ • Guidelines (Recommended best practices) │ ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │ RISK MANAGEMENT LAYER │ │ • Risk Identification │ │ • Risk Assessment │ │ • Risk Analysis │ │ • Risk Treatment (Accept, Transfer, Mitigate, Avoid) │ │ • Risk Register │ ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │ COMPLIANCE LAYER │ │ • Regulatory Requirements (GDPR, HIPAA, PCI-DSS) │ │ • Industry Standards (ISO 27001, NIST CSF) │ │ • Internal Compliance Programs │ │ • Audit Preparation and Execution │ ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │ THIRD-PARTY RISK LAYER │ │ • Vendor Assessment │ │ • Due Diligence │ │ • Contractual Agreements (SLAs, MOUs) │ │ • Ongoing Monitoring │ ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │ SECURITY AWARENESS LAYER │ │ • Training Programs │ │ • Phishing Simulations │ │ • User Guidance │ │ • Incident Reporting Channels │ └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘`

### LEVEL 2 — Governance Documents View

`┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ POLICY HIERARCHY │ │ • Executive-Level: Information Security Policy │ │ • Department-Level: Access Control Policy │ │ • System-Level: Password Policy │ │ │ │ STANDARDS │ │ • Mandatory Technical Requirements │ │ • Configuration Standards │ │ │ │ PROCEDURES │ │ • Step-by-Step Instructions │ │ • Change Management Process │ │ │ │ GUIDELINES │ │ • Recommended Practices │ │ • Non-Mandatory Guidance │ └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘`

### LEVEL 3 — Risk Management Process View

`IDENTIFY RISKS ↓ ASSESS LIKELIHOOD AND IMPACT ↓ CALCULATE RISK SCORE (Likelihood × Impact) ↓ DETERMINE RISK TREATMENT OPTION ↓ IMPLEMENT CONTROLS (If Mitigating) ↓ DOCUMENT IN RISK REGISTER ↓ MONITOR AND REVIEW ↓ REPORT TO STAKEHOLDERS`

### LEVEL 4 — Compliance Framework View

`┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ REGULATORY FRAMEWORKS │ │ • GDPR (Data Privacy - EU) │ │ • HIPAA (Healthcare - US) │ │ • PCI-DSS (Payment Cards - Global) │ │ • SOX (Financial Reporting - US) │ │ • CCPA/CPRA (California Privacy) │ ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │ INDUSTRY FRAMEWORKS │ │ • ISO 27001 (Information Security Management) │ │ • NIST Cybersecurity Framework │ │ • NIST SP 800-53 (Security Controls) │ │ • SOC 2 (Service Organizations) │ │ • CIS Controls │ ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │ INTERNAL COMPLIANCE │ │ • Policy Compliance Reviews │ │ • Control Testing │ │ • Evidence Collection │ └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘`

- Signed policy documents
- Risk register entries
- Vendor assessment reports
- Training completion records
- Audit findings and remediation

## 8. CORE QUESTIONS

### Security Governance

- What policies are required for security programs?
- How do standards differ from policies?
- Who approves security policies?
- How often are policies reviewed?
- What is the policy hierarchy?

### Risk Management

- What risk identification methods exist?
- How do we calculate likelihood and impact?
- What is the difference between risk appetite and risk tolerance?
- When do we accept vs. mitigate risk?
- What goes into a risk register?
- How do we conduct business impact analysis (BIA)?

### Compliance

- What compliance frameworks apply to our organization?
- How do we map controls to requirements?
- What constitutes compliance evidence?
- How often must we re-assess compliance?
- What are the consequences of non-compliance?

### Third-Party Risk

- How do we assess vendor security posture?
- What contracts protect against vendor risk?
- How do we monitor ongoing vendor compliance?
- What happens when a vendor breaches?

### Security Awareness

- What training content is required?
- How often should training occur?
- How do we measure training effectiveness?
- What is the phishing training program?

### Audits

- What is the difference between internal and external audits?
- What is an attestation?
- How do we prepare for an audit?
- What are audit findings and how are they remediated?

## 9. MISSION OPERATING MODEL

Mission 08 follows this cycle:

`BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS ↓ POLICY DESIGN ↓ POLICY WRITING ↓ STAKEHOLDER REVIEW ↓ APPROVAL ↓ IMPLEMENTATION ↓ TRAINING ↓ MONITORING ↓ AUDIT/ASSESSMENT ↓ REMEDIATION ↓ CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT ↓ SECURITY+ MAPPING`

Mission 08 differs from Missions 01–07 by emphasizing:

- Management over technology (Policies, not firewalls)
- Organizational over individual (Enterprise-wide, not system-specific)
- Process over implementation (Methodology, not configuration)
- Oversight over execution (Governance, not operations)
